Private home-automation project

A quieter, smarter way to discourage patio birds.

Patio Bird Deterrent is a personal, noncommercial application that uses camera events and image classification to distinguish birds from people and dogs before playing a brief deterrent sound.

Read the privacy policy
01

Detect

A local smart-home trigger starts verification when relevant patio activity occurs.

02

Classify

Selected camera frames are checked for birds, people, and dogs. Dark or unusable images are discarded.

03

Respond

A short owl sound may play locally only when a sufficiently confident bird-only detection is made.

Personal use only

This application is operated solely by its owner for use at a private residence. It is not offered as a public service, does not display advertising, and does not sell personal information.
